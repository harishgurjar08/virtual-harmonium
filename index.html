🎵 How to Play AR Virtual Harmonium

Experience playing a real Indian Harmonium in Augmented Reality right from your browser! You can play using your webcam with AI hand tracking, or via physical keyboard and mouse.

✋ 1. Hand Tracking (AR)

Click Start AR Camera and allow webcam access. Position your hand so your fingers are visible. Point and lower your index or middle finger onto the 3D keys to play notes!

🎼 2. Sargam Swaras

The keys represent the 8 primary Indian Classical swaras: Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni, Ṡa (सा, रे, ग, म, प, ध, नि, सां) with realistic double-reed resonant sound.

🪕 3. Tanpura & Bellows

Turn on Tanpura for continuous classical drone accompaniment. Switch bellows to Manual to pump air with your left hand or the pump button!

🎓 4. Practice Mode

Learn step-by-step with structured Indian Classical Alankars and songs like Raghupati Raghav. Follow the glowing keys!
